Spectre Thread

A single line through every tile of a pattern that never repeats.

The marked tiles

Every tile in the picture is one of these: the same shape, carrying one stroke of the thread from one edge, through the centre, to another. Pick one to see where it is used. The number is its share of all the tiles.

One shape
Every piece is the same 14-sided tile, the spectre, found in 2023. It covers the plane only in patterns that never repeat.
One line
The thread enters each tile once, crosses to a neighbour through a shared edge, and finishes every cluster before it leaves.
Eight at a time
Tiles group in eights, and the groups group in eights. A route through eight pieces, reused at every size, makes the whole thread.
Thirty routes
Hilbert's curve needs one route in four rotations. This pattern has no symmetry to lean on, so it takes thirty.
